Naoyuki Koike Laboratory

DIFFERENTIAL GEOMETRY / TOPOLOGY / GEOMETRIC ANALYSIS

My research focuses on submanifold geometry in symmetric spaces, and on mean curvature flow and inverse mean curvature flow — geometric flows closely related to the Ricci flow used in the resolution of the Poincaré conjecture. I am also interested in connections with general relativity and gauge theory.

Research Themes

Overview
1

Submanifolds in symmetric spaces of compact type

Reduction to the study of submanifolds in an infinite-dimensional Hilbert space via a Riemannian submersion, exploiting the flatness and absolute parallelism of the Hilbert space.

2

Submanifolds in symmetric spaces of non-compact type

Reduction via complexification (anti-Kaehler submanifolds) to the study of submanifolds in an infinite-dimensional anti-Kaehler space.

3

Mean curvature flow and inverse mean curvature flow in symmetric spaces

Collapse, divergence and convergence behaviour of submanifolds evolving under (modified) mean curvature flow equations.

4

Topology of hypersurfaces via mean curvature flow with surgery

Defining mean curvature flow with surgery for certain hypersurfaces and applying it to study their topology.
